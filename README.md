## How I build

```text
Business problem
      ↓
Workflow and data mapping
      ↓
Explainable rules and AI assistance
      ↓
Human review and safe boundaries
      ↓
Measurable operational outcome
```

- Start with the user’s workflow, not the model
- Make recommendations explainable and editable
- Keep consequential actions behind human approval
- Use synthetic demo data and protect credentials
- Design for CRM, API, and operational handoffs
- Document architecture, limitations, setup, and testing
