## Selected projects


[CRMArena-Pro Gate](https://github.com/ganjikrishna/crmarena-pro-gate): AI agents working in a CRM can be asked for data they should not reveal. I built and measured a pre-answer authorization gate (Gate Spec GS-2 v1.0) on Salesforce AI Research's CRMArena benchmark, B2B single turn tasks. Refusals of confidential requests rose from 0 of 75 to 42 of 75, while workflow scores held at 47 of 50 and policy scores at 10 of 100 in both runs. One request type, confidential company knowledge, still only reached 8%, and the repo states that plainly with the rest of its limits. Published November 13, 2026.

[Signet](https://github.com/ganjikrishna/signet-qwen25): editing a language model using only plus and minus signs. On a sealed test, story degeneration in Qwen2.5-0.5B fell from 86.0% to 69.1% (p = 0.0002) after sign-only edits to the weights, with no training run. The repo reports the method, the sealed results, and where the approach hits its limits. November 2026.

RustCRM: an open CRM base written in Rust, with role based and field level permissions built in. It is in testing now and will be published here under Apache 2.0.


## Earlier work


[Fraction Compressor](https://github.com/ganjikrishna/fraction-compressor-v0.4) compresses the difference between a base model and its fine-tune into a patch file of 19 MB, against 988 MB for the full change, at about 0.31 bits per weight. It closed 23.2% of the fine-tune gap on 96 questions. It is lossy, and a distilled LoRA baseline beat it. Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, October to November 2026, which ended without a win. That work is complete. The earlier version is at [fraction-compressor](https://github.com/ganjikrishna/fraction-compressor).
